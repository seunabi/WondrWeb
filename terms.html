Terms of use.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Using Wondr

Wondr helps you discover nearby places and plan walking or cycling routes. You agree to use the app lawfully, respectfully, and in a way that does not put yourself or others at risk.

2. Maps and route information

Routes, distances, directions, place information, and estimated times are provided for planning convenience. They may be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, or affected by changes to roads, paths, access, weather, construction, or Apple’s mapping data. Check the real-world conditions before and during a trip.

3. Safety

You are responsible for choosing whether a route is suitable. Follow local laws, signs, access restrictions, traffic rules, and your own judgement. Do not use Wondr while driving or in any situation where looking at your device would be unsafe.
